### 1. Box League Format

**Format:** Round-robin within each division. Each player plays all other players in their division once. Please aim to complete all games by end of day **Sunday November 29th 2026**.

---

### 2. Match Format & In-Game Rules

* **Target Duration / Games:** Play until one player has won 10 games (so a winning score would be 10-5, 10-3 etc).


---


### 3. Time Expiration (1-Hour Slot Limitations)


* **Hard Stop:** When court booking time expires, play ends immediately.


* **Unfinished Games:** Any game underway when time expires does not count towards the score unless the deciding point was completed before time was called.


* **Result Stands:** The score at time expiration is the official match result (e.g. 10–5, 10–3, etc.).

---

### 4. What happens if neither player reaches 10 games won in the allotted time?

If time runs out before either player has won 10 games, then please add games to the winning score in order to bring it to 10, and then add the same number of games to the losing score.

* **Example** a match stands 8–5 and the hour runs out. Add 2 games for both players, bringing the final score to 10-7.
